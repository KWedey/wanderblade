# Wanderblade — Vision

*One blade, one long road, and a whole realm of monsters between you and legend.*

**Working title:** Wanderblade
**Platform:** iOS / Android (TypeScript + web UI, wrapped with Capacitor)
**Status:** Pre-production (M0) — see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)

## The fantasy

You are a lone wandering swordfighter crossing an entire fantasy realm on foot — sunlit greenwood giving way to torchlit ruins, misty marsh, iron hills, ember wastes, and the glowing dragon peaks at World's Edge. Power growth is *felt*, not abstract: monsters that once bloodied you die in a single flashing strike as you march into new country. And World's Edge is not reached in one march — it is conquered across many **New Roads**, each pushed farther by the legend of the last.

## The session

Designed for 30–90 second check-ins, several times a day:

1. Open → **"Back on the Road"** recap: leagues traveled offline, loot earned, frontier advanced.
2. One tap collects the trophy sack (loot fountain, numbers fly).
3. Spend on a few glowing upgrades — one to five depending on time away, each a single tap, never a stat comparison. Even an 8-hour return fits the 90-second budget.
4. Optionally: challenge a boss gate when the Readiness meter glows **Ready**, snatch a trailside glint, or investigate a roadside discovery.
5. Close on a rising number.

Offline progress is the beating heart: the hero keeps walking and fighting while the app is closed, and returning must always feel like a payoff — never a chore, never a punishment.

## Design pillars

1. **You are the hero.** One growing character the player identifies with. Never a manager, never a base to babysit.
2. **Heroic adventure tone.** Vibrant and dangerous — torchlit ruins, bright skies, monster-filled wilds. Never grimdark, never cozy-cute.
3. **Classic, executed excellently.** Auto-battle → loot → upgrade → push further. The game's identity comes from its transforming world and its feel, not from a gimmick. It must be fully legible to someone who has never played an idle game.
4. **Numbers go up, collections complete.** Exponential power dopamine (damage, gold, leagues) plus long-horizon completion: Bestiary, gear sets, zone stars.
5. **Check-ins stay light.** No loadout math, no spreadsheet min-maxing. Every decision in a session is makeable in seconds.

## Reference bar

- **Egg Inc / Idle Miner Tycoon** — production polish; a lively world you enjoy watching.
- **Idle Slayer** — the optional active-play layer atop an auto-running hero (catching coins, chests, bonus moments) and a prestige economy that makes resetting feel like power, not loss.
- **Classic side-scrolling auto-battlers** — structural legibility; the proven loop.

## Anti-goals (each rejected explicitly during concepting)

- No manager/overseer gameplay; no town-management layer (a view-only trophy hall at most).
- No grimdark tone — no corpse walls, curses, or despair.
- No design that leans on a single clever twist to be interesting.
- No forced long active sessions; no min-max depth as a progression requirement.
